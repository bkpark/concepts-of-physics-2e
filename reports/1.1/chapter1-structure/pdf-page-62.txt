We see in Equation 2.79 that centripetal acceleration is greater at high speeds and in sharp curves (smaller radius), as you have
noticed when driving a car. But it is a bit surprising that ac is proportional to speed squared, implying, for example, that it is four
times as hard to take a curve at 100 km/h than at 50 km/h. A sharp corner has a small radius, so that ac is greater for tighter
turns, as you have probably noticed.
