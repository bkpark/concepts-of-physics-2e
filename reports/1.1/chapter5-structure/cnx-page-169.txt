Discussion
Because the product of f multiplied by λ equals a constant, the smaller f is, the larger λ must be, and vice versa.
The speed of sound can change when sound travels from one medium to another. However, the frequency usually remains the
same because it is like a driven oscillation and has the frequency of the original source. If vw changes and f remains the
same, then the wavelength λ must change. That is, because vw = fλ , the higher the speed of a sound, the greater its
wavelength for a given frequency.
