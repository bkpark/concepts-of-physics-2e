# Chapter 9 question-set proposal

**Status: author-approved and applied to maintained source on 2026-10-03; published in preview 69. Answer checks remain repository records, excluded from the hosted textbook.**

Recommend **55 questions**, comprising 46 retained/revised existing questions and 9 new questions. Existing-set disposition: 5 retain, 41 revise, 98 remove, 12 merge. The count follows coverage rather than a quota. Preserve the inline Section 9.11 Check Your Understanding item.

## Review basis

Reviewed all 156 current question prompts against the maintained section content and the September 27 MyOpenMath export. Inspected the current homework families, relevant essays, and lecture coverage. The crosswalk identifies 76 Chapter 9 records; many are timed or archived variants, not separate additions. Exact export records and assessment membership are retained privately in mom-review-source.json. No source code was executed, no external image was imported, and no live MyOpenMath course was edited.

The question text below is complete proposed wording. Current numbers refer to preview 68. Internal answers and checks are in answer-checks.md, not a proposed public answer key. Stable existing IDs will be preserved for retained/revised questions. New questions will receive new IDs after approval.

## Decisions to notice

- Keep a manageable mix of sign/direction reasoning, proportional predictions, sketches, and short calculations. Remove repeated substitution chains, medical-outcome calculations, and advanced square-charge equilibrium variants.
- Move current Exercise 69 (battery capacity) to 9.10 and current Exercise 72 (two-contact voltage difference) to 9.12.
- Add one self-contained zero-force-region task. Its source uses a diagram; the proposal defines all locations and charges in words and coordinates instead.
- No new images are required. Revised field-line questions ask students to sketch; revised switch and conductor questions specify their setups in words. Do not delete any source media until all surviving body/CYU references have been checked during implementation.
- Treat current 143 as a correction priority, not merely pruning: it presents touching a power line as a voltage check. [OSHA 1910.333(b)(2)(iv)](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.333) instead specifies verification by a qualified person using test equipment. Also replace current 137’s false no-shock premise.
- Household-load arithmetic is explicitly an idealized comparison, not electrical-installation advice or an exact breaker-trip model. [OSHA electrical safety training](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/2019-04/Basic_Electricity_Materials.pdf) distinguishes overcurrent and ground-fault protective devices.
