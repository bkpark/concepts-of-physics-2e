# Learning objectives review for version 1.1

**Review update:** All 36 reviewed sections have now been approved and applied, including the maintainer’s individual wording changes and decisions. The original audit below records the pre-edit baseline; the approved lists and proposals.json record the final objectives. Published in preview 154.

Reviewed the 321 learning-objective items in all 121 sections with objective lists against the current maintained section content. The full collection contains 141 pages: the other 20 are the preface, 15 chapter introductions, and four appendices. Appendix C has a descriptive abstract rather than a list of learning outcomes. No objective lists are proposed for these front-matter or reference pages.

There are proposals for 36 sections: 23 corrections or scope clarifications, 10 coverage improvements, and 3 choices about emphasis. This was the initial audit scope; source objectives have since been updated as recorded above. The approved changes are now published in preview 154.

The comparison uses section prose, equations, worked examples, and captions as the basis for what is taught. A topic appearing only in a chapter-end exercise does not establish section coverage. This is an objectives-alignment review, not a new fact-check of every claim in the prose. Generic verbs such as “understand” were not treated as content mismatches when their topics were supported. Suggested additions identify major omissions; they do not attempt to turn the objectives into an exhaustive outline.

Each entry below shows the complete current and proposed objective lists for that section. Unchanged objectives are retained in the proposed list. The coverage improvements and emphasis choices are separately reviewable; they need not be accepted along with the corrections.
