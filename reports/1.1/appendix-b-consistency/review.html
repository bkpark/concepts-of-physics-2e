Approved decisions: retain selected identifiable lines; replace ambiguous/vague entries with a few dominant emissions; use photons per 100 parent decays; use DDEP consistently for emissions even where its branching fractions differ from NUBASE2020. Beta endpoints calculated from the LNHB schemes for K-43 and Rb-86 are identified below. Parent/daughter delays and conversion-electron distinctions are explained in the textbook notes.
