    1    Decay Scheme

        40K is a natural isotope with an isotopic abundance of 0.011668 (8) %. It disintegrates by β− emission
    to the ground state of 40Ca at 89.56 (7) %, by electron capture to the 1460-keV level of 40Ar at 10.34 (7) %,
    and to the ground state of 40Ar by electron capture at 0.098 (25) % and by β+ at 0.00103 (13) %.
