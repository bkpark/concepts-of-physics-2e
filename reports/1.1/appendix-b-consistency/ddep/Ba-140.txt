   1    Decay Scheme

        Ba-140 decays by beta minus emission to various excited levels of La-140.
   The activity ratio La-140/Ba-140 at time t (for initially pure Ba-140) is given by:

                                                                       T1                         T1×T2).
                                                                    T1−T2(1−e−t×ln(2)×T1−T2

   whereT1 is the half-life of Ba-140 andT2 is the half-life of La-140.
   At equilibrium (t≥ 19 d) the activity ratio is simply:T1/(T1−T2) = 1.1516± 0.0005 .
