Transformers do what their name implies—they transform voltages from one value to another. For example, many cell phones, laptops, video games, and power tools and small appliances have a transformer built into their plug-in unit (like that in [reference]) that changes 120 V or 240 V AC into whatever voltage the device uses. Transformers are also used at several points in the power distribution systems, such as illustrated in [reference]. Power is sent long distances at high voltages, because less current is required for a given amount of power, and this means less line loss, as was discussed previously. But high voltages pose greater hazards, so that transformers are employed to produce lower voltage at the user’s location.
