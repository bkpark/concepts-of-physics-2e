Another set of reactions that result in the fusing of hydrogen into helium in the Sun and especially in hotter stars is called the carbon cycle. It is 12C+1H →13N+γ,13N →13C+e++ve,13C +1H →14N+γ,14N+1H →15O+γ,15O →15N+e++ve,15N+1H →12C+4He. Write down the overall effect of the carbon cycle (as was done for the proton-proton cycle in 2e−+41H→4He+2ve+6γ). Note the number of protons ( 1H) required and assume that the positrons ( e+) annihilate electrons to form more γ rays.
