## Scope notes

Only exact i/f subscripts are counted in the main inventory. Compound state labels are listed separately. Other endings such as prof, ref, and eff are words/abbreviations, not state labels. Content-MathML identifiers were also checked for underscore or Unicode-subscript encodings; none added cases. No running-index use was found among these exact i/f subscripts.

The source inventory preserves context and source XML for each occurrence. A few imported bases contain entire products (e.g. NkBT with a final-state subscript) or an equals sign alongside KE; these are separate structural issues, not italic-state-label exceptions.
