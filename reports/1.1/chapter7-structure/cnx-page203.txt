Density
Density is mass per unit volume.

where ρ is the symbol for density, m is the mass, and V is the volume occupied by the substance.
In the riddle regarding the feathers and bricks, the masses are the same, but the volume occupied by the feathers is much
greater, since their density is much lower. The SI unit of density is kg/m3 , representative values are given in Table 8.1. The
metric system was originally devised so that water would have a density of 1 g/cm3 , equivalent to 103 kg/m3 . Thus the basic
mass unit, the kilogram, was first devised to be the mass of 1000 mL of water, which has a volume of 1000 cm3.
