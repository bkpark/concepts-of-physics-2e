The displacement x(t) , the velocity v(t) , and the acceleration a(t) of simple harmonic motion sketches out sinusoidal
functions as a function of time. That is, if you sketch out sin(t) and cos(t) , with t in seconds, for example, what you graph will
appear similar to the actual graphs of x(t) , v(t) , and a(t) . You just need to scale them horizontally and vertically, so that they
have correct period and amplitude, respectively.
